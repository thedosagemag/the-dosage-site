General information for Victoria, not legal or building advice. The deposit and payment percentages are the standard ones for a contract to build all stages; your contract sets yours. These are the rules as of October 2026. Victoria's new domestic building contract laws are set to start on 1 December 2026, and the government has proposed new deposit and progress payment limits for homes, which it consulted on until 16 September 2026. If you sign a contract from 1 December 2026, check Consumer Affairs Victoria for the rules that apply to you.
